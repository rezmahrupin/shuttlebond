How ordering works

  1. 1

    Add items to your cart

    Pick quantities from the shop. Your order list updates in the cart panel.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    We confirm pickup or delivery

    We'll reply to arrange a time and place. Feather shuttles are made up fresh, so allow a day or two for larger orders.
